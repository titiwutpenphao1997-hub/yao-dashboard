How these figures are defined

On hand
Lot-level stock, excluding Damaged / Expired (shown separately per product). A Live tag means the figure is confirmed against the YAO Qty Reference real-time report; otherwise it's the daily workbook figure.
Open PO
Remaining qty on POV lines still at status P/O. Partly-received (Arriving) lines are excluded here and shown in the product detail instead.
Lots
Every in-stock lot, from the Stock Aging report. "Latest lot" = most recent received date, or last arrival record if there's no stock.
Units
Each product's own unit (103 of 112 codes = cartons). Planned receipts on workbook sheets are forecasts, not stock.

How shelf life is estimated

Confirmed
TTR, YSP, SANJ and Others(Japan) lots encode an expiry date directly (e.g. 10102028 = 10 Oct 2028) — read as-is, no table applied.
Estimatedpending QA
Penta lots (e.g. II17) encode the production date: letter 1 = year, letter 2 = month, digits = day. Expiry = production date + 12 months (OEM Penta rule), or +6 months for Marudai. Not yet confirmed by QA for Yamasa-brand items made at Penta — marked "Est." until QA sets the correct shelf life per SKU.
Risk tiers
% of shelf life left — OK ≥80% · Watch 70–80% · Alert 60–70% · Critical 0–60% · Expired <0%. A product's badge shows its worst lot.

Data source

Source
Compiled from the "Delivery and Billing Terms" workbook (6 sheets): customer list, billing with delivery, billing by post, billing by email, billing by P'On, and document attachment terms for delivery, including the change history.
Name matching
Short names and nicknames used in each sheet are matched to the master customer register. Entries that cannot be matched are marked Not in register. Please check the names in the source file.
No special terms
Customers in the register with no terms row in any sheet are treated as having no special terms on record (standard terms apply).
